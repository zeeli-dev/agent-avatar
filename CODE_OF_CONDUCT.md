# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, give and accept feedback on the work rather than the person, and leave no room for harassment of any kind.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and ban participants who break this code.
